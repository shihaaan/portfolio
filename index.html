About

UX, IT and cars

I spent three-plus years as a UX designer in Sri Lanka before moving to New Zealand to study. My master's research looked at prompt suggestions in generative AI interfaces like ChatGPT, and how they help people get started.

Today I work in IT support for Ebbett, looking after users, devices and systems. It brings together IT, UX and cars, the three things I love most, and I design internal tools and dashboards along the way.

Outside work, I love using Claude to turn ideas into real things. The Uncover Project is my passion project, two websites about the places I call home, made simply because I wanted them to exist.

Cars are my biggest passion.

I notice the details most people walk past: the line of a panel, the shape of a wheel, the way light moves across paint. SS96 MEDIA is where I photograph them, for people who believe cars have soul. And whenever I can, I'm out on track with my car club.
