How I work on these

Small scope, done properly

Plan the content first

Every site starts as a content plan: who it's for, what they need to know, and in what order. The layout follows from that.

Write things that last

History, culture and places don't go out of date, so the sites stay useful without a publishing schedule to keep up.

Credit every source

Facts come from trustworthy sources, and every photo is copyright-free and credited to the person who took it.

Build with Claude

I love building things with Claude. I use it to shape content plans, write code and test ideas quickly, then I review and refine every page myself. Each site ships as a real product with versions.

About

UX background, IT day job

I spent three-plus years as a UX designer in Sri Lanka before moving to New Zealand to study. My master's research looked at prompt suggestions in generative AI interfaces like ChatGPT, and how they help people get started.

Today I work in IT support, looking after users, devices, applications and infrastructure. I still bring UX into that work, designing internal tools and dashboards for the teams I support.

Outside work, I love using Claude to turn ideas into real things. The Uncover Project is where that comes together: my design practice, plus an AI-assisted UX process I'm building and testing end to end.
